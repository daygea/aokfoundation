Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet our obligations under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic technical information that any website receives.

  • When you contact us — your name, email address, phone number, and whatever you put in your message.
  • When you apply to the Academy — the information requested on the application form, which is hosted on Google Forms. See Academy applications below.
  • When you donate — see Donations and payments below. Importantly, we never receive or hold your card details.
  • Automatically — your IP address, browser and device type, and which pages you visited. This is ordinary web traffic information and we use it only to understand how the site is being used.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to you, and to assess and administer Academy applications — because you asked us to, or because it is necessary to take steps you requested.
  • To introduce Academy participants to partner employers — on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To acknowledge donations and keep proper financial records — because we are legally required to.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it — on the basis of our legitimate interest in running the site well.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate, and only as far as they need it to do that job:

  • Our payment provider, which processes donations and holds the payment data.
  • Google, which hosts our application form and the map on our contact section.
  • Partner employers, for Academy participants only, in connection with a specific opportunity, and only where you have agreed.
  • Our website host and email provider.

Some of these providers are based outside Nigeria, which means your information may be transferred and stored abroad. Where that happens we rely on the provider’s own data protection commitments, as permitted under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to two years after we last heard from you.
  • Academy applications: for the application cycle, and afterwards only if you asked us to keep your profile on file for future opportunities. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Donation records: as long as Nigerian financial and tax record-keeping rules require.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar technologies set by the services embedded in our pages — principally Google Maps on the contact section, and our email and analytics tools. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though the map may not display.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, and we limit access to the people who need it. No website or online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk.

Children

Our programmes serve young people, and some of our beneficiaries are under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child through this website without the involvement of a parent, guardian or school. If you believe we hold a child’s information that we should not, contact us and we will remove it.

Donations and payments

Donations are processed by Paystack, a licensed payment provider. When you click “Donate”, you leave our website and complete the payment on Paystack’s own secure page.

We never see, receive or store your card number or bank credentials. Those are handled entirely by Paystack under their own security standards and privacy policy. What we receive is confirmation that a donation was made, along with the name, email address and amount needed to acknowledge it and keep our records.

You may also give by direct bank transfer using the account details in the footer of this site.

Donations are voluntary gifts, not purchases, and are generally not refundable. If you believe a donation was made in error or without authorisation, contact us at info@aokfoundation.org and we will look into it and put right anything that is genuinely a mistake.

Academy applications

Applications to the AOK Foundation Academy are submitted through a form hosted on Google Forms, and the responses are stored in our Google account.

We use your application to assess eligibility, to run the programme, and — for participants who complete it — to introduce you to partner employers. We share your profile and your work with an employer only in connection with a specific opportunity.

If you would like us to stop holding your application, or to stop referring you to employers, email info@aokfoundation.org and we will act on it.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • ask us to delete your information, where there is no legal reason for us to keep it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on your consent;
  • receive your information in a portable form; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you are not satisfied with how we have handled your information.

To exercise any of these, email info@aokfoundation.org. We will respond within 30 days.
